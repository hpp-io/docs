# FAQ

## Wallet users

### Does becoming a smart account change my address?
No. Email sign-in uses [EIP-7702](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7702): your existing address starts
using smart-account code, and the address stays the same. (Only browser wallets such as MetaMask get a
new address — see [How it works](./how-it-works.mdx#same-address-eip-7702).)

### What happens after my free operations are used?
Gas is paid in USDC.e from your balance — you never need ETH. The wallet shows the fee before you
confirm; it is the gas cost at the current ETH price plus 5%, at least 0.001 USDC.e. x402 payments and
payments your agents make are signatures, not transactions, so they cost no gas at all.

### Does HPP hold my keys or my money?
No. Your key stays with the sign-in provider on your device, and your USDC.e stays in your own account.
HPP only pays (or charges) gas for operations you sign.

### I lost my device. Is my account gone?
No. Sign in with the same email on a new device and you get the same account.

### Can I revoke an agent from another device?
Yes. The **Agents** page lists agents issued in this browser, and it also lets you revoke a permission
issued elsewhere.

### What can an agent do with the permission I give it?
Only pay x402 services in USDC.e, up to the limit and before the expiry you chose. It cannot move your
other funds or change your account. See
[the security model](./agent-permissions.mdx#security-model-of-payment-delegations).

## Developers

### Do I need HPP's SDK?
No, any ERC-4337 client works against `aa.hpp.io/rpc`. The SDK saves you from several HPP-specific traps —
see [Reference → If you bypass the SDK](./reference.mdx#if-you-bypass-the-sdk).

### Can my users pay gas in USDC.e without a policy from HPP?
Not yet. Paying gas in USDC.e goes through HPP's token paymaster, which needs a policy. Without a policy,
the account pays its own gas in ETH.

### How do I get gas sponsorship on Mainnet?
Email [support@hpp.io](mailto:support@hpp.io) — see [Gas sponsorship → Get access](./gas-sponsorship.mdx#get-access)
for what to include. Develop on HPP Sepolia with the sandbox policy in the meantime.

### Is it safe to use `policyId` and `anchorId` from the browser?
The `policyId` is not a secret — limits are enforced by HPP's paymaster, per anchor. The `anchorId` is
what limits are counted against, so **your backend** should decide it after the user logs in; never let
users type or choose it.

### Why can one account send only one operation at a time?
Operations from the same account share a nonce, so each waits for the previous one. Batch calls into one
operation, or use up to 4 nonce lanes — see
[Agent permissions → Throughput](./agent-permissions.mdx#throughput-one-account-one-operation-at-a-time).

### Payment delegation or session — which do I need?
A **payment delegation** if an agent pays x402 services. A **session** if a bot or backend sends its own
operations to specific contracts. [Agent permissions](./agent-permissions.mdx) compares them.

### Which EntryPoint does HPP use?
Both v0.7 (`0x0000000071727De22E5E9d8BAf0edAc6f37da032`, used by Kernel and the SDK) and v0.8
(`0x4337084D9E255Ff0702461CF8895CE9E3b5Ff108`).
