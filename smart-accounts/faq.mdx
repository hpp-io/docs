# FAQ

## Wallet users

### Does becoming a smart account change my address?
No. Email sign-in uses [EIP-7702](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7702): your existing address starts
using smart-account code, and the address stays the same. (Only browser wallets such as MetaMask get a new
address — see [How it works](./how-it-works.mdx#same-address-eip-7702).)

### What happens after my free operations are used?
The network fee is taken in USDC.e from your balance — you do not need ETH in the wallet. The wallet shows
the most the fee can be before you confirm; you are charged the actual amount, usually less. Free operations
are also limited per day and per month, and those limits may change during early access.

### What costs a fee and what does not?
Sends, setting up agents, and revoking agents are **operations** (free while your free operations last, then
a small USDC.e fee). Paying an x402 service — by you or by your agent — is only a signature and has **no fee**.

### Who holds my key? Can HPP move my money?
Your key is managed by [Privy](https://www.privy.io), the sign-in provider; HPP does not store it. HPP can
only cover (or charge) the fee for operations **you** sign — it cannot move your USDC.e.

### I lost my device. Is my wallet gone?
No. Sign in with the same email on a new device and you get the same wallet. If you lose access to the
**email** itself, you lose the wallet — turn on two-factor authentication for your email, and see
**Settings** for exporting your key.

### How do I revoke an agent I added on another device?
Open **Agents → Revoke a payment delegation that is not in this list** and paste the HPP_PAYMENT_DELEGATION
value you saved when you issued it.

### What can an agent do with the permission I give it?
Only pay x402 services in USDC.e, up to the limit and before the expiry you chose. It cannot move your other
funds or change your account. See [the security model](./agent-permissions.mdx#security-model-of-payment-delegations).

### Where do I ask for help?
In [HPP Telegram](https://t.me/aergoofficial). Never share your email code or private key with anyone.

## Developers

### Do I need HPP's SDK?
No — any ERC-4337 client can use `aa.hpp.io/rpc`, within these limits: sponsorship goes through HPP's
paymaster only, sponsorship is for EntryPoint v0.7, and accounts must be Kernel, SimpleAccount, or EIP-7702.
The SDK also saves you from several HPP-specific traps — see
[Reference → If you bypass the SDK](./reference.mdx#if-you-bypass-the-sdk).

### Can my users pay gas in USDC.e without a policy from HPP?
Not yet. Paying gas in USDC.e goes through HPP's token paymaster, which needs a policy. Without a policy, the
account pays its own gas in ETH.

### How do I get gas sponsorship?
Open a [Gas sponsorship request](https://github.com/hpp-io/hpp-aa-sdk/issues/new/choose) on GitHub — see
[Gas sponsorship → Apply](./gas-sponsorship.mdx#apply). Start on HPP Sepolia.

### Can I put `policyId` and `anchorId` in my frontend?
Be careful with anchors. An anchor ID is all the paymaster needs to count an operation against that user's
quota, so treat it like a session token: never derive it from public data, and prefer sending sponsored
requests from your backend or through a backend proxy. See [Keep anchors private](./gas-sponsorship.mdx#policies-and-anchors).

### Why can one account send only one operation at a time?
Operations from the same account share a nonce, so each waits for the previous one. Batch the calls into one
operation with `sendCalls([...])`.

### Payment delegation or session — which do I need?
A **payment delegation** if an agent pays x402 services. A **session** if a bot or backend sends its own
operations to specific contracts. [Agent permissions](./agent-permissions.mdx) compares them.

### Which EntryPoint does HPP use?
v0.7 (`0x0000000071727De22E5E9d8BAf0edAc6f37da032`) — used by Kernel, the SDK, and sponsorship. The bundler
also accepts v0.8 (`0x4337084D9E255Ff0702461CF8895CE9E3b5Ff108`) for self-paid operations.

### Where do I report a bug?
[hpp-io/hpp-aa-sdk issues](https://github.com/hpp-io/hpp-aa-sdk/issues). Never post private keys, anchors,
or policy IDs.
