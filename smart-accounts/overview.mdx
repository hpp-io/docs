# Smart Accounts on HPP

A plain wallet is a private key: whoever holds it can do anything, and every action costs gas in ETH.
A **smart account** puts rules in code instead. On HPP that means a user can sign in with an email,
send their first transactions without owning any ETH, and give an AI agent a budget it cannot exceed —
enforced on-chain, revocable at any time.

HPP runs the whole [ERC-4337](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4337) stack itself — bundler,
paymaster, and smart-account contracts — behind one endpoint, `https://aa.hpp.io`, and ships a
ready-to-use wallet on top of it.

:::caution[Early access]
Smart Accounts on HPP Mainnet are newly launched. Sponsored (free) operations are limited and may change,
and there is no uptime guarantee yet. Start on HPP Sepolia, and use small amounts on Mainnet.
:::

## What you get

| | Plain wallet (EOA) | Smart account on HPP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Getting started** | Install an extension, write down a seed phrase, buy ETH for gas | Sign in with email. Your address is ready in about five seconds |
| **Gas** | Every action needs ETH | A limited number of operations are covered by HPP; after that, gas is paid in **USDC.e** — no ETH in the wallet |
| **Letting an AI agent pay** | Hand over a key that can spend everything | Grant a **limit** ("up to 5 USDC.e, for 30 days"). The chain enforces it; revoke with one click |
| **Several actions at once** | One pop-up and one fee each | One signature, executed together or not at all |

## How it relates to x402

[x402](/x402) is how an app or agent **pays** for an API call. Smart accounts change **what the wallet
is**. The two meet in one place: a smart account can grant an agent a *payment delegation*
([ERC-7710](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7710)), and the agent then pays x402 services from the
user's account — within the limit — without ever holding funds of its own.
