# HPP Smart Wallet

**HPP Smart Wallet** is a web wallet for HPP built on smart accounts. There is nothing to install and
no seed phrase: your email is the account. Your first operations cost nothing, and after that gas is
paid in USDC.e — you never need to buy ETH.

## Sign in

Open the wallet and choose **Continue with email or passkey**. Enter the code sent to your email.

![Signing in to HPP Smart Wallet](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-signin.png)

The first time, the wallet turns your address into a smart account. It takes about five seconds and
**HPP pays for it** — you will see *Setting up your account* and then your home screen. Your address does
not change; it is the same address your email login has always had
([EIP-7702](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7702)).

You can add a **passkey** later under **Settings**, so you can sign in with your device instead of an
email code.

## Your home screen

![The wallet home screen](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-home.png)

| Where | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Balance** | Your USDC.e, with how many **free operations** you have left |
| **Sponsored gas this month** | How much gas HPP has covered for you this month, and how many sponsored operations you used today |
| **Pay** · **Send** · **Receive** | The main actions — described below. **Receive** shows your address to copy |
| **Agents** | Agents you have given a budget to |
| **Recent activity** | Sends, payments, agent payments, and USDC.e you received |

The menu on the left has the same sections, plus **Activity** and **Settings**.

## Add USDC.e

Choose **Receive** and copy your address. Send USDC.e to it from another HPP wallet or an exchange, or
bridge it onto HPP — see the [Bridge guide](/community/bridge). On Sepolia, ask for test USDC.e through
[Official Links](/community/official-links).

:::warning
Only send **USDC.e on HPP** to this address. Tokens sent on another network will not arrive.
:::

## Send USDC.e

1. Choose **Send**, paste the recipient's HPP address, and enter an amount (**Max** sends everything
   except the fee).
2. **Review** shows the recipient, the network, the fee, and your balance after — before anything happens:
   - while you still have free operations, the fee is **none** — HPP pays the gas;
   - after that, the fee is the gas cost in USDC.e (at the current ETH price, plus 5%, minimum 0.001
     USDC.e). The wallet shows the most it can be; you are charged the actual cost.
3. **Send**, and approve the signature prompt. The transfer is done in a few seconds; **View on explorer**
   opens the receipt.

![Reviewing a send](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-send.png)

If the address is a contract rather than a personal wallet, the wallet asks you to confirm it can
receive USDC.e. Moving USDC.e off HPP is a bridge transfer, not a send.

## Pay an x402 service

[x402](/x402) services charge per call in USDC.e. To pay one by hand:

1. Choose **Pay** and paste the service's **x402 endpoint** (an `https://` URL). Open **Request body** only
   if the service asks for JSON input.
2. **Check price**. The wallet shows what the service is, who gets paid, and the price.
3. **Send**, and approve the signature prompt. A payment is just your signature — **no gas and no fee**.
   The service's answer appears below, with a link to the receipt.

![Paying an x402 service](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-pay.png)

Looking for services to pay? Browse the [x402 Explorer](/x402/explorer/browse) — each service page
shows its endpoint and price.

## Give an AI agent a budget

You can let an AI agent pay x402 services **from your wallet**, up to a limit you set. The agent never
gets your key and never holds your money.

1. Choose **Agents → Add agent**.
2. Give it a **Name** and paste the **agent's address** — the public address of the key the agent holds
   (the agent's tool shows it). Never paste a private key.
3. Pick a **Limit (USDC.e)** — the most it may spend in total — and a **Duration**.
4. Keep **Pay x402 services** selected and choose **Issue (one signature)**.
5. Copy the **HPP_PAYMENT_DELEGATION** value the wallet shows and put it in the agent's configuration.
   [Pay from an agent](./pay-from-an-agent.mdx) walks through this for Claude, Cursor, and other MCP hosts.

![Issuing a payment permission to an agent](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-agent-new.png)

The limits are checked **on-chain** for every payment: total amount, expiry, the exact amount and
recipient of each payment. They hold even if the agent misbehaves.

**Revoke** an agent at any time on the **Agents** page. It takes effect immediately and costs nothing.

:::info[Call contracts (session)]
**Agents → Add agent** also offers **Call contracts (session)** — for developers whose agent sends its
own transactions to specific contracts. See [Agent permissions](./agent-permissions.mdx).
:::

## Activity

**Activity** lists everything that moved through your account — sends, x402 payments, payments your
agents made, USDC.e you received, and account setup. Click an entry for details and a link to the block
explorer.

![The Activity page](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-activity.png)

Names and notes you see there are kept in your browser; the transfers themselves come from the chain.

## Good to know

- **The wallet does not hold your keys.** Sign-in is handled by an embedded-wallet provider; HPP never
  sees your key.
- **Free operations are per account.** Once they are used, gas comes from your USDC.e. Agent payments
  and x402 payments are signatures, so they cost no gas at all.
- **Lost device?** Sign in with the same email on a new device — you get the same account.
- **Agents issued on another device** do not appear in this browser's list, but you can still revoke
  them from the **Agents** page.
