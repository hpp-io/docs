# HPP Smart Wallet

**HPP Smart Wallet** is a web wallet for **HPP**, a blockchain network. It holds **USDC.e** — a US-dollar
stablecoin (Bridged USDC) on HPP — and lets you send it, pay for online services with it, and give an AI
agent a budget to spend from it.

There is nothing to install and no seed phrase: you sign in with your email. You do not need to hold ETH
in this wallet — your first few operations are covered by HPP, and after that the network fee is taken in
USDC.e.

:::warning[Stay safe]
- Only sign in at **aa.hpp.io** (or **aa-sepolia.hpp.io** for testing). Bookmark it.
- Nobody from HPP will ever ask for your email code, your private key, or your recovery details.
- Sends cannot be undone — not by you, not by HPP, not by anyone.
:::

## Sign in

Open the wallet and choose **Continue with email or passkey**, then enter the code sent to your email.

![Signing in to HPP Smart Wallet](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-signin.png)

The first time, the wallet sets up your account. It takes a few seconds and HPP pays the network fee for
it. Your address does not change — technically, your email login's address becomes a *smart account*
([EIP-7702](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7702)), which is what makes the free operations and agent
budgets below possible.

:::important[Your email is your wallet]
Sign-in and keys are handled by [Privy](https://www.privy.io), our sign-in provider; HPP does not store
your key. Signing in with the same email on a new device gives you the same wallet. **If you lose access to
your email, you lose access to the wallet** — turn on two-factor authentication for your email account.
**Settings** shows how to export your key from Privy if you want to move to another wallet, and lets you
add a **passkey**.
:::

## Your home screen

![The wallet home screen](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-home.png)

| Where | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Balance** | Your USDC.e |
| **N free operations left** | How many more operations HPP will pay the fee for. After that, fees are taken in USDC.e |
| **Sponsored gas this month** · **sponsored operations today** | The monthly amount and the daily number of operations HPP covers for you. When either runs out, fees are taken in USDC.e until the next day or month |
| **Pay** · **Send** · **Receive** | The main actions, described below. **Receive** shows your address to copy |
| **Agents** | Agents you have given a budget to |
| **Recent activity** | Sends, payments, agent payments, and USDC.e you received |

An **operation** is anything that changes your account on the network: a send, setting up an agent, or
revoking one. Paying an x402 service is not an operation — it is only a signature, and has no fee.

The menu on the left has **Home**, **Pay**, **Agents**, **Activity**, and **Settings**.

## Receive USDC.e

Choose **Receive** and copy your address. Share it with whoever is sending you USDC.e **on HPP**.

:::warning
Only USDC.e **on the HPP network** arrives in this wallet. Tokens sent on another network (Ethereum, Base,
Arbitrum, …) to this address cannot be recovered.
:::

On **Sepolia**, the testnet wallet uses test USDC.e with no real value — ask for some in
[HPP Telegram](https://t.me/aergoofficial).

## Send USDC.e

1. Choose **Send**, paste the recipient's HPP address, and enter an amount. **Max** sends everything except
   the fee.
2. **Review** shows the recipient, the network, the fee, and your balance after — before anything happens:
   - while you have free operations, the fee is **none**;
   - otherwise the fee is a small amount of USDC.e. The wallet shows the most it can be; you are charged
     the actual amount, which is usually less.
3. **Send**, and approve the signature prompt. The transfer is done in a few seconds; **View on explorer**
   opens the receipt.

![Reviewing a send](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-send.png)

Double-check the address — a send cannot be reversed. If the address is a contract rather than a personal
wallet, the wallet asks you to confirm it can receive USDC.e.

## Pay an x402 service

[x402](/x402) services are online APIs that charge a small amount of USDC.e per use. To pay one by hand:

1. Choose **Pay** and paste the service's **x402 endpoint** (an `https://` address the service gives you).
   Open **Request body** only if the service asks for JSON input.
2. **Check price**. The wallet shows the service, who gets paid, and the price.
3. **Send**, and approve the signature prompt. There is **no fee** — a payment is only your signature. The
   service's answer appears below, with a link to the receipt.

![Paying an x402 service](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-pay.png)

To find services, browse the [x402 Explorer](/x402/explorer/browse) — each service page shows its endpoint
and price.

## Give an AI agent a budget

You can let an AI agent (such as Claude) pay x402 services **from your wallet**, up to a limit you set. The
agent never gets your key and never holds your money.

1. Choose **Agents → Add your first agent** (or **+ Add agent**).
2. Give it a **Name** and paste the **agent's address** — the public address of the key the agent holds
   (the agent's tool shows it). Never paste a private key.
3. Pick a **Limit (USDC.e)** — the most it may spend in total — and a **Duration**.
4. Keep **Pay x402 services** selected and choose **Issue (one signature)**. The first time you add an
   agent, the wallet also sets up your account for agents — that counts as one operation.
5. Copy the **HPP_PAYMENT_DELEGATION** value the wallet shows into the agent's configuration, and **keep a
   copy somewhere safe** — you need it to revoke the agent from another device.

![Issuing a payment permission to an agent](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-agent-new.png)

Setting up the agent's side (Claude Desktop, Cursor, …) needs some technical steps —
[Pay from an agent](./pay-from-an-agent.mdx) walks through them.

The limits are enforced **by the network**, for every payment: the total amount, the expiry, and the exact
amount and recipient of each payment. The agent cannot go past them.

### Revoke an agent

On **Agents**, choose **Revoke**. It is one operation and usually takes effect within seconds; HPP normally
covers its fee. Then remove the old value from the agent's configuration.

To revoke an agent you added on **another device**, open **Agents → Revoke a payment delegation that is not
in this list** and paste its HPP_PAYMENT_DELEGATION value.

:::info[Call contracts (session)]
**Add agent** also offers **Call contracts (session)** — for developers whose agent sends its own
transactions to specific contracts. See [Agent permissions](./agent-permissions.mdx).
:::

## Activity

**Activity** lists everything that moved through your account — sends, x402 payments, payments your agents
made, USDC.e you received, and account setup. Click an entry for details and a link to the block explorer.

![The Activity page](/img/smart-accounts/wallet-activity.png)

Names and notes you see there are kept in your browser; the transfers themselves come from the network.
