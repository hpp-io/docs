# HPP Hub

HPP Hub is the developer portal for House Party Protocol — build, test, and publish
AI-powered apps and games without writing code, and access HPP's AI models.

> **Open the portal →** sign up free at **[hub.hpp.io](https://hub.hpp.io)** (Google or
> email) and start building in minutes.

Describe what you want in plain language and HPP Hub builds it for you, with a live
preview you can test and publish.

## What you can build

- **Web App** — dashboards, tools, trackers, and more
- **2D Game** — runners, RPGs, arcade games
- **3D Game** — mazes and simple 3D worlds
- **Web3 dApp** — on-chain apps on HPP _(in internal testing — not yet on the production Hub)_

## What's inside

- **Playground** — describe what to build, preview and publish it, and chat with leading
  AI models (Claude, GPT, Kimi, and more).
- **Wallets** — your Credit balance and your on-chain wallets on HPP Mainnet.
- **API Keys** — issue keys for [HPP Router](/hpp-router) and call HPP's AI models from
  your own code.
- **Credits** — pay-as-you-go, topped up with USDC.e on HPP Mainnet.

## Verifiable by design

Every AI response in the Playground is fingerprinted and recorded on the HPP chain, so you
can confirm it was never altered — _don't trust, verify_. Only the fingerprint goes
on-chain, so your conversations stay private.

## Who it's for

HPP Hub is built for **builders** — you don't need to be a developer. Membership is
required; sign up free with Google or email.
