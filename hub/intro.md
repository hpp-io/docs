# HPP Hub

**HPP Hub** is the developer portal for House Party Protocol — your single access point
to HPP's developer tools, including HPP Coder.

→ Open the portal at **[hub.hpp.io](https://hub.hpp.io)**

This guide walks through what HPP Hub offers and how to use it.

## In this guide

- **Getting Started** — accessing and signing in to HPP Hub
- **Developer Tools** — the tools available in the portal
- **HPP Coder** — using the coding LLM offered free through the Hub
- **FAQ** — common questions and troubleshooting

:::note
This guide is being expanded. New sections are added under the **HPP Hub** menu as they
are written.
:::
