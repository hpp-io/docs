# Playground

The Playground is where you build and test in HPP Hub. Describe what you want in plain
language, and HPP Hub generates it, builds it, and shows you a live preview.

## Build an app or game

1. From **Home**, choose what to make — **App**, **2D Game**, **3D Game**, or
   **Web3 dApp** — or just start typing in the Playground.
2. Describe what you want (e.g. _"a habit tracker with daily check-ins and streaks"_), or
   pick one of the **guided prompts** on Home if you're not sure where to start.
3. HPP Hub builds it and opens a **live preview** you can interact with.
4. Keep refining by chatting — ask for changes and the preview updates.
5. When it's ready, **publish** to share it. <!-- TODO: confirm publish flow against the live site -->

Your projects save automatically — continue from **Home** or the project list.

> **Note:** **Web3 dApp** is currently in internal testing and may not be available on the
> production Hub yet.

## Verify a response on-chain

Every response in the Playground is fingerprinted and recorded on the HPP chain. Use
**Verify** on a message to confirm the response hasn't been altered since it was created —
_don't trust, verify_. Only the fingerprint is stored on-chain, so your prompts and
responses stay private. <!-- TODO: confirm the Verify action label/location against the live site -->
