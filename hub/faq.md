# FAQ

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. HPP Hub is built for builders — you describe what you want and it's generated for you.

### Is HPP Hub free?

Signing up is free. Building and AI chat use **Credits**, which you top up as you go.

### How do I pay for Credits?

With **USDC.e on HPP Mainnet**. See [Wallets](./wallets).

### What can I build?

Web apps, 2D games, and 3D games. **Web3 dApps** are in internal testing and not yet
available on the production Hub.

### Which AI models can I use?

A wide range, including Claude, GPT, and Kimi models, plus free open models. The full list
and pricing is on the **[API Keys](./api-keys)** page in the Hub — these models are served
by [HPP Router](/hpp-router).

### Can I use the AI models from my own code?

Yes. Create an API key on the **[API Keys](./api-keys)** page and call the models through
[HPP Router](/hpp-router), HPP's OpenAI-compatible gateway.

### Are my conversations private?

Yes. HPP Hub records only a fingerprint (hash) of each AI response on the HPP chain so you
can verify it wasn't altered — your prompts and responses are not stored on-chain. See
[Verify a response on-chain](./playground#verify-a-response-on-chain).

### Need help?

Use **Support** in the Hub to get in touch.
