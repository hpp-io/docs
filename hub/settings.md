# Settings

Manage your account from **Settings**.

## Account

- **Email & password** — update your sign-in details
- **Google** — connect your Google account for one-click sign-in
- **Email preferences** — opt in or out of newsletters and event emails

## Security

Turn on **two-factor authentication (2FA)** with an authenticator app for an extra layer
of protection.

## Invite friends

Share your invite link to earn a reward — you and your friend each receive Credit when
they sign up. <!-- TODO: confirm current reward amounts against the live site -->

## Delete account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from the **Danger Zone**.
This cannot be undone.
